## C. Seller consultation script — one experienced advisor, several paths
**1. Open (20 sec):**
`Tell me what you'd like to accomplish with the property. Is your priority price, timing, simplicity, being able to stay, or something else?`
**2. Discover (2–4 min):** condition, current listing status, timeline, equity/debt information only when appropriate, constraints, need for repairs, preferred exposure, likelihood of buyer demand, willingness to host showings, what unsuccessful past attempts taught them.
**3. Reflect before recommending:**
`What I hear is [goals/constraints]. May I walk through the tradeoffs between the approaches we might use, including reasons one might *not* fit?`
**4. Compare without steering:** traditional MLS brokerage; auction/competitive-offer route where operationally available and buyer demand supports it; direct as-is purchase through a separate party where appropriate; independent stay-in-home resources if the seller's priority is not selling.
**5. Explain costs and obligations:** written representation/brokerage status; listing and auction terms, fees including any buyer premium, buyer agent participation, reserve vs absolute terms where applicable. No published fee schedule yet.
**6. The integrity question:**
`Even if we do none of these today, what would make this conversation useful to you?`
**7. Next step:** written recap, appropriate specialist and permissioned handoff. Do not promise response times until team capacity is confirmed.

## Live-site gates and business distinctions
- No promise of an available auction if not actually offered with approved terms and operational controls.
- No blanket 'same service level' without checking real signed agent roles, seller responsibilities and contact-to-close workflow.
- No fake auction testimonials or 'best agents', 'more buyers', 'fastest', 'highest price' unsupported superiority.
- No fee or buyer-premium numbers before the business chooses a lawful and accurate model. See [#10](https://github.com/roger812/rivet-auctions/issues/10) and [#11](https://github.com/roger812/rivet-auctions/issues/11).
- Do not assume `rivetre.com` is controlled by Rivet Auctions, or that any brokerage represented on that website is automatically involved in a Rivet Auctions property. Verify the actual listing agreement and scope before any joint advertising.
- No automatic IWCO marketing enrollment or contact data replication based solely on brand affiliation.
