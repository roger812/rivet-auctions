# Atlas | Unified Seller-Options Messaging System

> **Founder clarification, Nov 17:** Rivet Auctions is Roger's own company. No outside brokerage is automatically affiliated; listing-broker participation requires an actual agreement for the property. Marketing assets must not imply otherwise.
**Draft V1 · November 17, 2026 · Cross-brand creative source of truth · NOT APPROVED PUBLIC COPY**

## Executive thesis
**Rivet Auctions is Roger's independently owned auction business, intended to create additional seller relationships across his businesses.** Its auction method can be paired with separately contracted listing brokerage representation where appropriate. Brokerage-level care depends on the actual agreement and services delivered, not an automatic sponsorship or business ownership relationship.

**Entity boundaries:** Rivet Auctions is Roger's own auction business; Rivet Real Estate is a separate brokerage relationship that may participate for individual properties by agreement. MLS marketing is coordinated through the actual engaged listing brokerage, only where applicable. IWCO is a separate homeowner-options business. Referrals require accurate affiliations, permission and business-specific records.

**Umbrella thought:** `Your home. Your circumstances. More than one way forward.`
This is a working positioning idea, not an approved public slogan or promise that all choices suit every property.

### What makes us different (hypothesis)
Not that Texas has no real-estate auctioneers. Competitors exist. The potential opening is making a *genuine* auction method feel like **familiar professional brokerage representation** rather than an unfamiliar liquidation. Founder reports roughly $5,000 invested historically in auctioneer capabilities; do not mistake sunk cost for a low ongoing marketing/CAC or staff-labor requirement.

### Audiences we must keep separate
1. **Proactive seller** — wants an informed comparison before selecting a method. Do not frame them as desperate.
2. **Stalled/expired listing seller** — may want a fresh plan, without being promised a quick sale or higher price.
3. **As-is/complex property seller** — needs realistic tradeoffs: conventional exposure, auction route where appropriate, direct offer options where eligible.
4. **Buyer and buyer's agent** — needs clear, written submission rules, authorized status information, and a reliable response channel. A displayed high price is not necessarily the best offer on terms.

### Brand roles, language and intended outcomes
| Business | Reason to enter | Primary promise (only when operationally supported) | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Rivet Real Estate** | Trusted advisor and full-service sale | Professional guidance to compare viable selling approaches, with a plan appropriate to the home | `Talk through my selling options` |
| **Rivet Auctions** | Founder-owned auction process | Explore whether a structured competitive-offer auction would fit the property; brokerage representation and MLS exposure where separately arranged | `Explore an auction-style selling plan` |
| **IWCO** | As-is/direct purchase and homeowner choices | Explore cash, conventional sale or staying put where appropriate without pressure; inquiry isn't an offer | `Explore my options` |

### Core language and behavior
**Use:** choice, clarity, as-is where relevant, appropriate selling plan, written rules, property-specific recommendation, MLS coordination subject to actual listing status, seller-defined terms under signed agreement, your agent can participate under stated rules, experience and care, disclosure before a decision.

**Avoid:** 'fastest sale', 'market value guaranteed', 'everyone is bidding', 'no more commission', 'no buyer premium', 'reserve means you can always reject', 'highest bid equals best offer', 'agents hide offers', 'foreclosure pressure', 'unlimited exposure', 'the only company', broad 'days on market are exploding' unless dated local evidence, 'no extra fee' because pricing is still undecided.

### Assurances we can discuss conceptually, not promise until proven
- A human consultation and truthful suitability assessment.
- Listing strategy, photography/property presentation, offer/agent coordination, and contract-to-close support.
- Reserve or seller-confirmation conditions **only if they match the signed auction/listing terms**.
- How and when offer-status disclosures are permitted, verified, timestamped and communicated.
- Staff acknowledgement and response timing **only when verified and staffed**.

### Visual brand system
- **Image:** relatable *daylight* Central Texas house, lived-in and well-maintained; not luxury mansion, twilight photo, foreclosure notice, distressed/embarrassed homeowner portrait, or false before/after.
- **Creative direction:** warm human faces when licensed/permissioned; natural light; generous whitespace; real estate credibility. Make the sales process feel understandable; avoid aggressive auction paddles/hammer as the only dominant motif.
- **Trust evidence:** linked, accurately attributed Rivet Real Estate reviews (not invented auction outcomes); recognizable broker identity; MLS relationship described precisely; clear policies and staff availability; visible 'why auction may not fit'.
- **Photos:** use licensed original media and authorized testimonials. Do not transfer IWCO customer photos/reviews or BDL private property media without explicit permission.

### Brand architecture and cross-referral rules
An interested seller enters one **clearly identified** brand. At consultation, present *only appropriate* alternatives. If a seller chooses an affiliated business, explain relationship, who will contact them, and obtain relevant permission before transferring their identifiable information. Keep business/source attribution and permissions intact. An auction option must actually be available; do not use it as bait for an ordinary listing.

**Consumer-facing affiliation draft (review):**
`Rivet Auctions is Roger's auction business. For a property with an agreed listing-broker relationship, its auction process may work with professional brokerage representation and MLS exposure where appropriate. We will explain the people involved, actual terms and costs before you choose.`

**IWCO handoff draft (review):**
`A cash offer may not be the best fit for every home. With your permission, we can help you explore a separate brokerage consultation through Rivet Real Estate.`

### Evidence standard + dissent
- 'Auction is rare' and 'other auctioneers are expensive/dry' are *founder impressions*, not validated statewide facts. Competitor price audit required before public comparison.
- 'More choice increases trust' and 'auction increases signed listings' are testable hypotheses, not observed results. More choices may also increase confusion.
- The primary financial measure is **incremental Rivet/IWCO contribution after marketing, failed listings, all staff costs and fair value of owner labor**, net of cannibalized ordinary listings. No internal auction↔brokerage fee double count.
- Preserve rival hypotheses in [Atlas dissent #9](https://github.com/roger812/rivet-auctions/issues/9); commercial experiment in [#12](https://github.com/roger812/rivet-auctions/issues/12).
