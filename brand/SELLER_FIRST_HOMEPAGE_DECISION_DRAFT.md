## The decision we're making
The founder reports that the hero house is too luxurious, and the message fails to reach a homeowner who has already listed without success. Create a truthful, effective, **seller-first** path for ordinary Central Texas owners who face repeated showings, waiting, possible price cuts, and uncertainty. This is a distinct product from an instant cash offer or a forced liquidation.

Rivet's proposed differentiator is **MLS reach, when part of the actual listing arrangement, paired with a structured competitive-offer/auction process with an agreed timeline and terms**. Our promise must be clarity and a professional process, not a guaranteed price, sale, time-to-close, or market outcome. The seller's actual reserve/confirmation rights require signed-contract verification before any wording ships.

## Priyanka's offer-first framework — proposed decision
**What a seller gets by clicking:** an **Auction Fit Review**: a short human-supported assessment of (1) their current listing/situation, (2) whether the competitive offer approach fits, (3) intended marketing and offer timeline, (4) reserve/confirmation options and fees, and (5) clear alternatives if it is not a fit. We are NOT promising an instant appraisal, cash offer, or guaranteed market value. Confirm operator capacity and turn-around times before stating an SLA.

**Offer hypothesis:** The clearest conversion promise is not "get the highest price" but "get an informed next decision instead of more open-ended waiting."

### Rejected until supported by transaction data
- "Get market value the fastest"
- "Guarantee the highest price"
- "Auction always gets more money"
- "Your home will sell in X days"
- "You can always say no to any bid" (may be false for absolute or other binding auctions)
- "Days on market are growing" as a broad undated market claim

These can be discussed as hypotheses only with recent geographic evidence, qualified contract terms, and substantiation. Neither concept uses a fabricated market statistic or artificial countdown.

## Plain-language education blocks
**Problem:** "Another week of waiting can mean more uncertainty, carrying costs, and a difficult decision about price. A new strategy should address that uncertainty—not just relabel your listing."

**What Rivet does:** "When offered under your actual agreement, your property is marketed through the MLS while interested buyers receive clear auction/offer instructions, a defined process, and a stated decision window. Your Rivet team explains how buyer questions, offer verification, and response timing work."

**Myth:** "Does auction mean I have to take less?"  
**Answer:** "Not necessarily. Auction is a method for inviting competitive bids; it does not itself determine the property's value. Some auctions use an agreed reserve or seller-confirmation process. If bidding does not meet the applicable reserve, the property is not automatically sold at that price. Terms, fees, and any post-auction negotiation rights must be clear in writing. In an **absolute auction**, no reserve protects the seller; we must never suggest otherwise."

**Honest trade-off:** "Competitive bidding can fail to meet the reserve, may draw limited buyers, and may not suit every property. Your actual marketing costs, listing agreement, auction terms, commissions, and closing obligations matter."

**MLS clarity:** "Rivet Auctions operates alongside the MLS and licensed real-estate representation when specified in the seller agreement—not as a replacement property search or a mysterious separate auction marketplace."

**Buyer/agent path:** Distinct "I am a buyer or agent" action that explains where to find the MLS listing, how to inquire, how offers are delivered, and how verification/timing work. Do NOT require a phone call as the only way to learn the rules; provide a concise process page and short explanatory video script after operations validation.

## Suggested homepage sequence
1. Hero A/B: a recognizably ordinary home or homeowner, one seller CTA and educational secondary action.
2. Pain acknowledgment: open-ended days on market, showings, price-cut fatigue, carrying cost; no fake aggregate statistics.
3. "The Rivet Auction Advantage through the MLS": four concise steps, with verified action owners/timelines.
4. "Auction does not mean giving your home away": reserve vs absolute explainer, one **clearly hypothetical** numeric example only after approved terms.
5. "Is this right for me?": when it helps, when it may not; transparent fees/contract.
6. A 60–90 second "How this works through the MLS" video storyboard and accessible transcript, subject to factual review. No pretending buyer instructions or notification automation are live.
7. Seller inquiry and separate buyer/agent inquiry action; no public property catalog and no unverified highest-offer ticker.
8. Evidence/reviews only with permission and support; display broker/auction disclosures where required.
