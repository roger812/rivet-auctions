# Rivet Auctions → Rivet Real Estate — Same-Table Contracting & Care Journey
**Founder-directed sequence · 2026-11-17 · Internal operating concept, NOT a contract template or public promise.**
Related: [deferred legal issue #11](https://github.com/roger812/rivet-auctions/issues/11#issuecomment-6961561491), [asset PR #13](https://github.com/roger812/rivet-auctions/pull/13), [commercial pilot #12](https://github.com/roger812/rivet-auctions/issues/12).

## Founder-approved **business intent** (legal review still pending)
Rivet Auctions is **Roger's own company** and the seller's initial point of entry. The seller **first signs an agreement with Rivet Auctions**. Once that agreement exists, Rivet Auctions coordinates/partners with **Rivet Real Estate for that property**. In **the same face-to-face appointment, immediately afterward**, the seller signs a **separate second agreement hiring Rivet Real Estate as listing brokerage**. The customer experiences this as a coherent supported process with two transparently identified parties and two actual agreements. This is **not** a referral a week later and **not** an automatic brokerage affiliation arising from Rivet Auctions' existence.

### What the homeowner should feel
**One coherent consultation; two clearly explained engagements; continuous professional care.**
- Sellers should understand *why* there are two agreements and what each company delivers.
- It should feel like a connected offering, not a surprise handoff or an opaque extra charge.
- Brokerage representation is not implicitly established by contacting Rivet Auctions.
- Buyer-agent participation and property marketing details come from the real engaged broker and auction documents.
- If either party declines to sign or the arrangement is not suitable, do not say the process/MLS exposure is automatically available; have an approved alternate path.

## Draft customer explanation — private concept until counsel approves
> Rivet Auctions is the auction-service business you first engage. If you decide this selling approach is right for you, we'll review the Rivet Auctions agreement and explain how it works with professional listing services. At the same meeting, you'll also review a **separate listing agreement with Rivet Real Estate**. Each agreement covers a different role; we'll explain the services, costs, obligations and responsibilities before you sign.

Avoid **'everything is included for free'**, **'no need for a listing agreement'**, **'Rivet Auctions already is the listing brokerage'**, **'the same company signs both agreements'**, **'Rivet Real Estate is automatically appointed'**, and any claim that an unapproved contract gives an unconditional right to cancel or reject auction outcomes.

## How the asset package should change
**Rivet Auctions landing page:** Lead with genuine seller choice and confidence. Give a concise 'How representation works' FAQ: auction agreement first, distinct Rivet Real Estate listing engagement in the same appointment when both are offered and agreed. Do not imply the legal forms are ready for signatures now.
**Rivet Real Estate seller page:** When describing Rivet Auctions, acknowledge that the homeowner separately engages Rivet Auctions for that option, while Rivet Real Estate is retained separately as listing brokerage. Avoid legal-entity conflation or assuming one site publishes the other.
**IWCO:** Distinct lead source, brand, privacy permissions. An introduction to Rivet Auctions or Rivet Real Estate must not automatically enroll or transfer customer information.
**Agent brief:** Define a single named coordination contact at the appointment, but exact offer-receipt/response duties follow the two actual agreements.

## Legal review agenda (deferred during private design; required before live use)
1. Whether an auctioneer-only agreement can be executed **before** seller enters a listing representation agreement under applicable Texas law, including agency and material information disclosures.
2. Any legally necessary condition, contingency, dependency, right to withdraw, termination and refund if the second listing agreement is not signed or cannot be performed.
3. Whether/how Rivet Auctions and Rivet Real Estate enter a documented partnership/service agreement, and correct division of permitted real estate and auction functions.
4. Accurate identification of responsible broker and auctioneer in both agreements, advertising, MLS entries and buyer communications; no inferred outside brokerage sponsorship.
5. Seller and buyer premiums, services, compensation routing between companies/agents, who handles money, lender/title treatment and contract-to-close obligations.
6. Customer access to informed choice before either agreement; no misleading 'same-day two contracts' pressure or hidden fees.

**Implementation gate:** No invented legal documents, no new fee schedule, no real customer signatures or public sale/auction operation until qualified Texas counsel and the responsible licensed parties review and accept the process. This document is a statement of commercial intent, not a legal conclusion.
