## 6. Source-to-conversion attribution
**Primary measured event:** accepted server-confirmed homeowner request with correct business/source, followed by genuine human consultation, appropriate signed listing and closed revenue. **NOT** click, form-open, ad impression or raw contact count. Track whether an auction-led prospect **actually would not otherwise** have chosen Rivet through cohorts and interviews, not a marketing guess.
- UTM convention (proposed for RA campaigns only): `utm_source=google&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=rivet_auction_seller_austin_v1` with real page route after approval.
- Keep IWCO Google URL/measurement as already configured until fresh inspection; do not overwrite tags from memory.
- Keep ChatGPT ad click attribution and source separate; no automatic cross-business lead access. Measurement in platform ad accounts needs platform-specific proof.
- Counterfactual: ordinary listing message vs auction-option message to comparable eligible homeowner groups, then compare qualified consultations, genuine signed Rivet agreements, seller fit, cost and owner time. Advertise services, not specific individual housing properties in ChatGPT Ads.
