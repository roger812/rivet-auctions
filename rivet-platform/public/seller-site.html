THE IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

An auction doesn't have to mean giving your home away.

Some auctions use a reserve or seller-confirmation condition; others are absolute. These arrangements have different obligations. We'll explain the actual property-specific rules before you choose, rather than promise a right to refuse every bid.

A defined process can help organize buyer participation, but it cannot create demand where none exists or guarantee a price, closing date, or number of bidders.

FOR BUYERS & THEIR AGENTS

Clear participation.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Clarity before commitment.

Do I have to auction my home?

No. A consultation is not an agreement to sell. We'll explain whether an auction option appears suitable and what alternatives may be appropriate.

Will an auction automatically sell my home for less?

No particular result is guaranteed. Outcomes depend on buyer interest, terms, marketing and market conditions. Some formats have reserve or confirmation rules; we will explain the actual written terms.

Who represents me if we choose the auction option?

Rivet Auctions is a separate auction company. Under the planned process, you first engage Rivet Auctions, then separately engage Rivet Real Estate as listing brokerage at the same appointment. The listing agreement identifies the actual representation and services.

Will the property be on the MLS?

MLS exposure is available only where arranged through the properly engaged listing brokerage and permitted by the property's actual listing and auction rules. We won't imply that every inquiry is already an MLS listing.

How much does this cost?

Listing and auction fees, and any buyer premium if applicable, must be disclosed in the property-specific documents before you agree. No numerical fee schedule is finalized in this development preview.

Can my buyer use their own agent?

Yes, subject to the property's actual participation instructions and representation agreements. Compensation, if applicable, is negotiated in writing; this page makes no fixed compensation offer.

What if we decide a conventional listing is better?

Then the recommendation should reflect that. The purpose of exploring the auction route is to find an appropriate selling method—not force a particular transaction.
